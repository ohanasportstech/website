This Privacy Policy should be read together with our [Terms of Use](/docs/terms-of-use/). If you use Kai on behalf of a tennis club, academy, facility, business, institution, or other organization (a “Customer”), additional terms may apply to that Customer under our [Terms of Purchase](/docs/terms-of-purchase/) or another written agreement with Ohana Sports.

#### 1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Kai and which features are enabled.

**a. Account and Contact Information**

When you create or use a Kai account, we may collect:
- your email address;
- a username, display name, or other account identifier, if provided;
- authentication identifiers, tokens, and account-security information;
- account status, preferences, and settings; and
- information used to determine or document age eligibility or parental or guardian authorization, where applicable.

We do not need your home address or telephone number to create a standard Kai User account unless a particular feature, legal requirement, support process, or business relationship requires it.

**b. Training and Activity Information**

When you use Kai, we may collect information about your use of drills and training features, such as:
- drills, playlists, and training activities used or completed;
- practice activity, duration, and history;
- drill settings, ball-machine commands, and related configuration information;
- Module and ball-machine connection events; and
- other performance or training information that you choose to create or that the Service generates as part of your use of Kai.

Your personal training and performance history is generally associated with your Kai account rather than owned by the Customer or facility where you use Kai.

**c. User Content**

You may create, upload, submit, store, or share content through the Service, including custom drills, playlists, titles, notes, comments, and similar materials (“User Content”). We collect and process that content so we can provide, store, synchronize, support, and, when you choose, share it.

Please do not include personal, confidential, sensitive, or legally protected information about another person unless you have the right and authority to provide it. Users should not use free-text or sharing features to submit personal information about a person under 16.

**d. Customer, Facility, and Administrative Information**

If you use Kai through a Customer, or if you are a Club Administrator, we may collect information such as:
- the Customer or facility with which your account is associated;
- your administrator role and permissions;
- Module identifiers, activation status, connection activity, and subscription-related settings;
- information about which Users connect to which Kai Modules; and
- records relating to Customer administration, support, and account management.

**e. Device, Technical, and Usage Information**

We and our service providers may automatically collect technical and usage information, such as:
- device type and device identifiers;
- operating-system and app versions;
- IP address and approximate location derived from IP address;
- login, connection, and event timestamps;
- features, screens, or drills accessed;
- session frequency and duration;
- navigation and interaction patterns;
- crash, diagnostic, and performance information; and
- security, fraud-prevention, and troubleshooting logs.

We do not use Kai to collect precise GPS location unless a feature clearly requires it and appropriate notice or permission is provided.

**f. Communications, Support, and Safety Reports**

If you contact us, we may collect your name, email address, message contents, attachments, troubleshooting information, and other information you choose to provide. This includes support requests, equipment or Service safety reports, account-security reports, privacy requests, and communications relating to copyright or other legal matters.

Please do not include medical or health information in support or safety communications beyond what is reasonably necessary to describe an equipment or Service safety issue.

Information submitted in a copyright notice or counter-notification may be disclosed as described in our [Copyright and DMCA Policy](/docs/copyright-policy/).

**g. Billing and Transaction Information**

Most individual Users do not pay Ohana Sports directly. If you act on behalf of a Customer in connection with a Kai purchase or subscription, a payment processor may collect payment-card or other payment-method information. Ohana Sports may receive related billing and transaction information, such as billing contact details, payment status, subscription status, invoice information, or limited payment-method information. We generally do not receive or store complete payment-card numbers.

Some Kai features allow you to share drills, playlists, or other User Content through a shareable link. If you choose to share content, information included in that content may be accessible to anyone who has the link.

Where the Service allows you to make shared content private or revoke a link, doing so will prevent future access through that link after the change takes effect. It cannot retrieve or delete screenshots, exports, copies, or other records previously made by people who had access.

You are responsible for deciding what information to include in content you choose to share. Do not include personal, confidential, or sensitive information that you do not want others to see.

We do not sell personal information.

We may disclose personal information in the following circumstances:

**Customers and authorized administrators.** If you use Kai at a Customer’s facility, the Customer and its authorized administrators may receive certain account, device, Module-connection, administrative, or usage information relating to use of Kai at that facility. A Customer does not own your personal Kai account or automatically receive your general personal training history merely because you use Kai at its facility.

**Legal, safety, and rights protection.** We may disclose information where we reasonably believe disclosure is required by law or legal process, or is reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of Ohana Sports, our Users, Customers, service providers, or others.

**Copyright and intellectual-property matters.** We may disclose information submitted in a copyright notice, counter-notification, or other intellectual-property complaint as reasonably necessary to administer our complaint process and comply with applicable law.

**Business transactions.** Information may be disclosed in connection with an actual or proposed merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, bankruptcy, or similar business transaction, subject to appropriate protections where required.

**With your direction or consent.** We may disclose information when you direct us to do so or otherwise provide valid consent.

#### 7. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some web browsers offer a “Do Not Track” (“DNT”) signal. There is no uniform industry standard for interpreting DNT signals, and the Service does not currently use DNT signals as a privacy control. We do not use Kai personal information to track Users across third-party websites or online services for third-party targeted advertising.

Global Privacy Control (“GPC”) is a browser- or device-based signal that can communicate certain privacy opt-out preferences. Because Ohana Sports does not sell personal information or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, a GPC signal does not currently change how we process your personal information. If our practices change so that applicable law requires us to honor GPC or another opt-out preference signal, we will do so as required.

Kai personal accounts and Service controls are intended only for people age 16 or older. You may not create a Kai account for a person under 16.

If you are 16 or 17 years old, or otherwise under the age of majority where you live, you may use Kai only with the authorization of a parent or legal guardian as described in our Terms of Use. Where we collect information to document or verify that authorization, we may collect the parent or guardian’s name, contact information, relationship to the User, authorization status, and records showing when and how authorization was provided.

The account-age restriction does not prevent a person under 16 from participating in a tennis lesson, clinic, practice, or other activity in which an authorized User operates Kai for other players, subject to appropriate supervision and facility rules. We do not intend to create personal Kai accounts or personal training profiles for those participants.

Kai is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information through individual Kai accounts from children under 13. If we learn that an ineligible person has created an account or that we have collected personal information in a manner inconsistent with this section, we may suspend or delete the account and delete or otherwise handle the information as required by applicable law.

A parent or legal guardian who believes that a minor has provided personal information to us in violation of this Policy may contact us at support@ohanasports.net.
